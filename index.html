ClipFarm

ClipFarm is a private tool that its owner runs on their own computer. It cuts short vertical clips from long videos and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel, Built on Risk.

It is not offered to the public. Only the owner's own Google account can sign in to it.

It uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to that one channel and to read that channel's video list and view counts. It does not access any other Google data.
